Vm = 1 and f = 1 Hz. The real part a of the phasor scales cos ωt and the imaginary part b scales sin ωt, so V = 1∠φ gives v(t) = cos φ·cos ωt + sin φ·sin ωt = cos(ωt − φ). With this mapping a counter-clockwise angle φ delays the wave: φ = 90° (V = j) is sin ωt, which peaks a quarter period after cos ωt. The red dot marks v(0) = a, the phasor's projection on the real axis.
